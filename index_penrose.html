Project "Spacetime Maps"

A collection of 10 feature-rich, standalone HTML simulations exploring the construction and interpretation of Penrose diagrams for various spacetimes.

Simulations

  • 1. Minkowski Spacetime

    An interactive guide to the conformal mapping of flat spacetime, showing how an infinite universe is mapped to a finite diamond.

  • 2. Eternal Schwarzschild Black Hole

    Explore the four regions of the maximal Schwarzschild extension, including the black hole, white hole, and two universes connected by a non-traversable wormhole.

  • 3. Black Hole from Gravitational Collapse

    Visualize the more realistic scenario of a black hole forming from a collapsing star, showing how the event horizon comes into being.

  • 4. Reissner-Nordström (Charged) Black Hole

    Journey through the infinite ladder of universes inside a charged black hole and learn how to avoid its timelike singularity.

  • 5. Kerr (Rotating) Black Hole

    A hybrid simulation showing the ergosphere and ring singularity of a rotating black hole, and the potential for closed timelike curves.

  • 6. de Sitter Spacetime

    Explore an accelerating universe and the nature of observer-dependent cosmological event horizons.

  • 7. Anti-de Sitter (AdS) Spacetime

    Visualize the "universe in a box" with its reflecting timelike boundary, a key concept in the AdS/CFT correspondence.

  • 8. Flat FLRW Universe

    See how the particle horizon grows over time in a standard matter-dominated, expanding universe.

  • 9. Closed "Big Crunch" FLRW Universe

    Visualize a universe with a beginning and an end, showing how all paths are trapped between the Big Bang and the Big Crunch.

  • 10. Traversable Wormhole

    Explore the physics of a Morris-Thorne wormhole and see how accelerating one mouth can lead to causality-violating time travel.
