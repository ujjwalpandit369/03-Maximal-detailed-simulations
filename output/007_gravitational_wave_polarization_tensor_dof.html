Overview for the Curious

Imagine spacetime as a vast, taut rubber sheet. A massive event, like two black holes colliding, creates ripples that travel outward across this sheet, much like a stone dropped in a pond. These ripples are **gravitational waves**. They are not waves that travel *through* space, but rather waves *of* space itself. As a wave passes by, it literally stretches and squeezes the space it travels through. This simulation shows what happens to a ring of free-floating particles as a gravitational wave travels directly "out of the screen" towards you. You can see how the two fundamental polarizations, **plus (+)** and **cross (×)**, deform the ring in different ways, providing a direct visual of Einstein's most profound prediction.
