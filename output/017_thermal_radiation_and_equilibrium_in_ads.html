017: Black Hole Thermal Equilibrium in AdS

Visualizing how a black hole and its Hawking radiation can find a stable balance inside a confining spacetime.

Temperature vs. Time

Overview for the Curious

In our universe, black holes are destined to die. They slowly "leak" energy via **Hawking radiation**, and because they get hotter as they get smaller, this process accelerates until they evaporate completely. But in the universe-in-a-box of Anti-de Sitter (AdS) space, this isn't the case. The AdS boundary acts like a perfect mirror, trapping the radiation. This simulation shows a black hole at the center of AdS, emitting radiation particles. These particles bounce off the walls and form a thermal gas. The black hole can then re-absorb particles from this gas. This sets up a dynamic process of emission and absorption. You can watch as the temperature of the black hole and the temperature of the gas eventually meet, and the system settles into a state of **thermal equilibrium**—a stable balance that is impossible in our own universe.
