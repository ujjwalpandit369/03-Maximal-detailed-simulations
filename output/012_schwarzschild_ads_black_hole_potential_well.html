012: The Schwarzschild-Anti-de Sitter Black Hole

Exploring particle orbits in the combined potential of a black hole and a confining spacetime.

Effective Potential Well

Particle Orbit (Poincaré Projection)

Overview for the Curious

What happens when you place a black hole inside the "cosmic fishbowl" of Anti-de Sitter space? You get a fascinating interplay between two different types of gravity. The black hole creates a familiar, powerful attractive force (`-1/r`) that wants to pull everything in. The AdS spacetime itself creates a gentle, confining force (`r²`) that wants to pull everything back to the center. The combination of these two forces creates a unique **effective potential well**. This simulation lets you explore that potential. Depending on a particle's energy and angular momentum, it can fall into the black hole, settle into a stable orbit, or even be trapped in an "unstable" orbit at the top of a potential hill. This model is a crucial tool for understanding black hole thermodynamics and the AdS/CFT correspondence.
