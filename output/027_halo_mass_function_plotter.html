027: The Halo Mass Function

An interactive plotter for one of the key theoretical predictions of modern cosmology.

Halo Mass Function: dn/dM vs. Mass

Overview for the Curious

How many galaxies like the Milky Way should there be in a given volume of space? How common are giant galaxy clusters? How rare are tiny dwarf galaxies? The **halo mass function** is the theoretical tool cosmologists use to answer these questions. It provides a precise prediction for the number density of dark matter halos of a given mass. This prediction depends sensitively on the fundamental parameters of our universe, like the total amount of matter and the initial "lumpiness" of the cosmos. This interactive plotter lets you explore the halo mass function. By changing the cosmological parameters with the sliders, you can see how the predicted abundance of structures—from small galaxies to massive clusters—changes. This is a powerful way to understand how the "cosmic census" of halos provides one of the strongest tests of our entire cosmological model.
