Overview for the Curious

What are temperature and pressure, really? At the microscopic level, they are not fundamental properties. They are **emergent phenomena** that arise from the collective motion of countless individual atoms or molecules. **Temperature** is a measure of the average kinetic energy of the particles—how fast they are jiggling. **Pressure** is the result of all those particles constantly colliding with the walls of their container. This simulation allows you to see this connection directly. It models a 2D "ideal gas" as a collection of particles bouncing off each other and the container walls. By observing the microscopic chaos, you can see the macroscopic laws of thermodynamics, like the Ideal Gas Law (`PV=NkT`), emerge in real-time. You can also see how particle speeds are not all the same, but follow the famous **Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution**.
