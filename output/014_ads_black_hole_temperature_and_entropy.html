Overview for the Curious

Black holes in our universe are cosmic destroyers; they have a "negative specific heat," meaning as they radiate energy away, they get hotter and evaporate faster in a runaway process. But what if a black hole lived inside the "cosmic box" of Anti-de Sitter space? The confining nature of AdS spacetime changes everything. This simulation plots the **Temperature** and **Entropy** of a black hole as a function of its size (horizon radius). You will see that unlike a normal black hole, an AdS black hole can be stable. Its temperature reaches a minimum value and then starts to *increase* as it gets bigger. This means it has a "positive specific heat" and can exist in stable thermal equilibrium with its surroundings, a key feature that makes it a perfect theoretical tool for studying quantum gravity.
