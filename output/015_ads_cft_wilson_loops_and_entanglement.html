Visualizing the holographic principle: how quantum properties on the boundary are encoded in the geometry of the bulk.

AdS Bulk Geometry

Overview for the Curious

The **holographic principle** is a radical idea suggesting our universe might be like a hologram: all the information about the 3D "bulk" space we live in could be encoded on a distant 2D surface. The AdS/CFT correspondence makes this idea concrete. It proposes a dictionary between a theory of gravity in the Anti-de Sitter (AdS) bulk and a quantum theory on its boundary. This simulation visualizes two key entries in that dictionary. You can choose a property on the boundary—like the **entanglement** between two regions, or the **potential energy** between a quark-antiquark pair—and the simulation will show you the corresponding geometric object in the bulk whose area calculates that property. It's a direct look at the machinery of holography, where questions about quantum information and particle physics are magically transformed into questions about geometry.
