Overview for the Curious

Galaxies grow by eating each other. More accurately, the vast dark matter halos they live in grow by merging with other halos. This process is the most dramatic and transformative event in a galaxy's life. This simulation lets you be the architect of such a cosmic collision. You can set up two dark matter halos, control their mass ratio and how directly they collide, and then watch the show. You will see **dynamical friction** in action, a gravitational drag force that causes the two halos' orbits to decay. You will see **tidal stripping**, where the larger halo tears matter from the smaller one, creating beautiful streams and tails. Finally, you will witness **violent relaxation**, the chaotic process where the two halos merge into a single, larger, and more spherical object. This is the fundamental mechanism of hierarchical structure formation.
