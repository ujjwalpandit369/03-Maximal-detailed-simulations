Overview for the Curious

Every object with mass in the universe pulls on every other object. This is Newton's law of universal gravitation. When you have just two bodies, like the Earth and the Sun, their motion is simple, predictable, and elegant—an ellipse. But what happens when you add a third body? Or a fourth? Or a thousand? The problem explodes in complexity. There is no simple formula that can predict the future. This is the famous **N-body problem**. This simulation is a numerical laboratory that solves the N-body problem by calculating every force on every particle at every moment in time. You can use it to build your own solar systems, watch star clusters interact, discover stable orbits like Lagrange points, or witness the beautiful chaos that often emerges when three or more bodies vie for gravitational dominance.
