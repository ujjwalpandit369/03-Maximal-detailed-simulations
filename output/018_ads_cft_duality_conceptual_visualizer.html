Overview for the Curious

The holographic principle suggests that our 3D universe could be a projection from a 2D surface. The AdS/CFT correspondence makes this idea concrete, providing a "dictionary" to translate between a world with gravity (the "bulk") and a quantum world without gravity on its boundary. This interactive visualizer is your guide to that dictionary. Choose a concept from the dropdown menu. As you move the slider, you will see two worlds change in unison. Watch as heating up the quantum particles on the boundary causes a black hole to appear and grow in the bulk. See how the spooky quantum entanglement between two regions on the boundary is perfectly measured by the area of a simple geometric surface in the bulk. This is a direct visualization of the magic of holography: complex quantum problems become simple geometry problems in a higher dimension.
