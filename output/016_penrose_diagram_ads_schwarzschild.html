Overview for the Curious

How do you draw a map of an infinite universe that contains a black hole, a region from which nothing can escape? Physicist Roger Penrose invented a clever technique to do just that. A **Penrose diagram** is a "conformal" map that squishes an infinite spacetime into a finite, manageable picture, while correctly preserving the paths of light rays (which always travel at 45-degree angles). This simulation provides an interactive Penrose diagram for a black hole inside the confining "box" of Anti-de Sitter space. By launching particles and light rays, you can explore the fundamental causal structure of this spacetime. You'll discover for yourself the meaning of an event horizon—the point of no return—and see why anything that crosses it is inexorably drawn to the central singularity, a region where space and time as we know them end.
