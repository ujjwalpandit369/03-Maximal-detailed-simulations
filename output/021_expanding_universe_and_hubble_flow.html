Overview for the Curious

In the 1920s, Edwin Hubble and Georges Lemaître made a startling discovery: almost every galaxy in the universe is moving away from us, and the farther away a galaxy is, the faster it appears to be receding. This observation, known as the **Hubble-Lemaître Law**, is the foundational evidence for the expansion of the universe. But does this mean we are at the center of a great explosion? No. The **cosmological principle** states that the universe is homogeneous and isotropic—it looks the same everywhere and in every direction. This simulation demonstrates this profound idea. You can see a grid of galaxies in an expanding space. Click on *any* galaxy to make it your home. You will see that from every single vantage point, the same law holds true: everything is moving away from you. This shows that there is no center to the universe; rather, the fabric of space itself is expanding everywhere, carrying the galaxies along with it.
