Eternal Inflation & Bubble Collisions

A Suite of 10 Detailed Physics Simulations

1. The False Vacuum & Quantum Tunneling

Visualizing the fundamental mechanism of bubble nucleation from a metastable vacuum state.

2. Single Bubble Expansion

Modeling the exponential expansion of a single true vacuum bubble within the false vacuum.

3. The Eternally Inflating Background

A view of the global structure, where inflating space expands faster than bubbles can fill it.

4. Head-on Two-Bubble Collision

Simulating the collision of two bubble universes and the resulting hyperbolic geometry.

5. Off-center Two-Bubble Collision

Exploring the asymmetric geometry and energy release from a glancing collision.

6. Multi-Bubble Percolation

Simulating the point where bubble nucleation overwhelms inflation, ending the eternal phase.

7. Gravitational Wave Production

An illustrative simulation of the quadrupolar gravitational waves generated by collisions.

8. Particle Production at the Bubble Wall

A microscopic view of the bubble wall converting vacuum energy into a shower of particles.

9. The Landscape of Vacua

A simulation of a complex energy landscape with competing vacua of different energy levels.

10. CMB Signatures of Bubble Collisions

Visualizing the circular "bruise" a collision could leave on the Cosmic Microwave Background.
