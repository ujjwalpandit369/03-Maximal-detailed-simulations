Project "Cosmic Canvas"

A collection of 10 feature-rich, standalone HTML simulations exploring the physics of topological defects in the early universe. Each page is a deep dive into a specific phenomenon, containing extensive explanatory text, interactive controls, live metrics, and analysis tools.

Simulations

  • 1. Domain Walls

    A simulation of domain wall formation in a 2D Ising model, illustrating Z₂ symmetry breaking. Watch as spin domains form and compete during a quench.

  • 2. Cosmic Strings

    Visualizes the Kibble mechanism for a U(1) symmetry breaking, leading to the formation of cosmic string defects in a 2D complex scalar field.

  • 3. Magnetic Monopoles

    Explore the "hedgehog" defects that arise from SO(3) symmetry breaking in a 3D vector field, representing the formation of magnetic monopoles.

  • 4. Textures (Skyrmions)

    A simulation of an unstable, space-filling texture defect. Watch as this global "knot" in the field untwists and decays into a uniform state.

  • 5. Hybrid Defects

    A kinematic simulation of a domain wall bounded by a cosmic string loop, showcasing the dynamics and eventual collapse of this composite object.

  • 6. String Network Evolution

    Watch a 2D network of cosmic strings evolve. See strings straighten, intercommute (reconnect), and pinch off loops to reach a stable "scaling" solution.

  • 7. Defect Annihilation

    A detailed simulation of a circular domain wall collapsing under its own tension, converting its potential energy into a radiating scalar field.

  • 8. CMB Signatures

    An illustrative simulation of the Kaiser-Stebbins effect, showing how a moving cosmic string can create a line-like temperature discontinuity in the CMB.

  • 9. Gravitational Lensing

    An interactive tool to visualize the unique lensing signature of a cosmic string, which produces double images of background objects without magnification.

  • 10. Electroweak Baryogenesis

    A schematic simulation of particle-antiparticle interactions at the boundary of an expanding bubble wall during the electroweak phase transition.
